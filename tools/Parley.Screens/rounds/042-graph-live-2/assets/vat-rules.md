# VAT on UK orders

Prices are shown tax-inclusive. VAT is worked out per line, then the lines are added.

| Class | Rate | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| standard | 20% | Everything not listed below |
| zero | 0% | Books, children's clothing |

## Rounding

Round each line's tax-inclusive price half up to the penny. Never round the total on its own:
the total is the sum of the rounded lines, so it always matches the invoice.

## Worked examples

1. A jumper at £24.99 net: £24.99 × 1.20 = £29.988, shown as **£29.99**.
2. A book at £8.50 net: zero-rated, shown as **£8.50**.
3. Shipping at £3.75 net: £3.75 × 1.20 = £4.50, shown as **£4.50**.

The order total is £29.99 + £8.50 + £4.50 = **£42.99**.
