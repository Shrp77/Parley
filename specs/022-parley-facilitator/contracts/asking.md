# Contract: what an agent is asked

Two texts, chosen by the project's Auto Accept switch **at the moment the question is sent** (research R7). Both
keep 020's two halves — what it owns, and what it does not own and who does — because the exclusion is the half
nobody else can supply.

## With Auto Accept on

Replaces 020's last paragraph, which would otherwise be untrue:

> Your answer becomes your ownership line as you write it, and every agent in this project reads that line when
> deciding who to ask. Answer in one paragraph. A person can review and change it later.

## With Auto Accept off

020's text, unchanged:

> A person will read your answer, may disagree with it, and may argue about it with you before anything is
> written. Nothing you say here changes any configuration by itself.

## Rules both keep

- One ordinary Request, from the facilitator, in a conversation named `owns-<role>-<stamp>` (020's form).
- **An answer counts only if it answers the facilitator's own Request** — never because a conversation's name
  starts with `owns-` (research finding 11).
- Both texts live in `Parley.Core`, because the broker sends them and the surfaces show them (research R3).
- No marker in the payload a surface could parse (020's rule, and §13's promise that a payload is inert data).
