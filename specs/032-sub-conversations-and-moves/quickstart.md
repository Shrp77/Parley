# Quickstart: checking it by hand

1. In a conversation, press **Spin off** on a message. The box holds the quote and only that author is ticked. Leave without sending: no conversation appears in the list.
2. Do it again, name it `disk-cleanup`, edit and send. The list reads `disk-cleanup - spinoff from <parent>`, and each conversation links to the other.
3. In a conversation with several exchanges, press **Move entries**, tick one message of a longer exchange, and continue. The page lists the whole exchange, says it is still open if it is, and names the files that stay. Press Cancel: nothing changed.
4. Confirm into a new name. The source says what left and the destination what arrived, each linking to the other, and the moved messages read exactly as before.
5. Move an open exchange. Its agent starts no turn. At its next collection `parley_inbox` carries a move notice, and its answer lands in the new conversation.
6. Move them back. Both moves stay on record, and the export of each says so.
7. Closed conversations and roadmap items' conversations are not offered as destinations. Type one's name as a new conversation: it is refused with a sentence and nothing changes.
